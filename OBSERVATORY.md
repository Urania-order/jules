# The Observatory

The Observatory is the subsystem responsible for monitoring the evolution of the ECO Co-SMOS ecosystem.

## Responsibilities

- **Measure Ecosystem Health:** Aggregating metrics from all subsystems (Ecology, Commons, Value).
- **Observe Evolution:** Tracking the birth of clusters and the emergence of new disciplines.
- **Detect Dormancy:** Identifying valuable knowledge that has lost resonance.
- **Measure Collaboration:** Assessing the quality of Human-AI cooperation and conflict resolution.

## Quarterly Observatory Reports

Every quarter, the Observatory automatically generates a comprehensive report covering:
1.  Ecosystem Health
2.  Knowledge Ecology & Adoption
3.  Living Cluster Status
4.  Human–AI Cooperation Quality
5.  Knowledge Resonance & Impact Ranking
6.  Research Progress & Hypotheses
7.  Reconstructed Lost Knowledge
8.  Beyond Consensus Discoveries
9.  Behavioral Collaboration Trends
10. Emerging Disciplines & Future Opportunities

## Knowledge Impact Ranking

The Observatory replaces popularity with an **Impact Ranking**. The Top 10 quarterly contributions are selected based on:
- Practical implementation count.
- Cross-cluster influence.
- Verified societal/environmental impact.
- Collaborative diversity.
- Long-term usefulness.

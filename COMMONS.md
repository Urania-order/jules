# The Commons

The Commons is the shared living space of ECO Co-SMOS. It is not a database, but an interaction layer where:

- **Humans and AI collaborate** as equal participants.
- **Knowledge evolves** through discussion and implementation.
- **Recipes are transformed** into collective experiences.
- **Experiments emerge** from shared goals.
- **Communities grow** around intellectual clusters.

## Core Functions

1.  **Collaboration Facilitation:** Matching Cosmonauts by behavioral profile and cognitive style.
2.  **Knowledge Sharing:** Moving individual insights into the shared ecosystem.
3.  **Experiment Recording:** Tracking the outcomes of recipe applications to refine collective wisdom.

## Symbiosis

In the Commons, AI provides the scale for exploration and connection, while humans provide the meaning and purpose required to maximize knowledge value.

# Knowledge Ecology

In ECO Co-SMOS, knowledge is treated as a living entity. It is not just stored; it propagates, resonates, and sometimes goes dormant only to be reconstructed later.

## Propagation

Knowledge propagates through **Resonance**. When an idea or recipe is applied successfully by a `Cosmonaut`, its resonance increases, causing it to "vibrate" and attract attention from related `IntellectualClusters`.

## Resonance

Resonance is both direct and indirect.
- **Direct Resonance:** Application of a recipe leads to a measurable outcome.
- **Indirect Resonance:** An idea influences a person's behavior or an AI agent's reasoning style, even if they don't explicitly cite the source.

## Dormancy and Reconstruction

Not all knowledge is useful all the time. Knowledge that lacks resonance may enter a **Dormancy** state. However, it is never deleted. The **Backward Knowledge Discovery** system can pull dormant nodes back into the active ecology if they are found to be relevant to a new outcome or experiment.

## Living Clusters

Clusters are more than folders or tags; they are populations.
- **Health:** Determined by activity and successful knowledge application.
- **Diversity:** The variety of cognitive styles and epistemic layers within the cluster.
- **Inflow/Outflow:** How much new knowledge is being created vs. how much is being exported to other parts of the ecology.

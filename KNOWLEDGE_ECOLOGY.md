# Knowledge Ecology

Knowledge in ECO Co-SMOS is treated as a living entity that undergoes continuous co-evolution.

## Lifecycle States

Knowledge moves through states, never reaching a final "static" form:
`Idea` -> `Discussion` -> `Recipe` -> `Experiment` -> `Practice` -> `Behavior Change` -> `Collective Experience` -> `Improved Knowledge`.

## Living Clusters

Intellectual clusters are living entities with:
- **Health:** Activity and successful application density.
- **Diversity:** Cognitive and behavioral variety of its population.
- **Resonance:** Influence on other clusters.
- **Inflow/Outflow:** The rate of knowledge exchange with the wider ecosystem.

## Pollination

Knowledge is propagated through pollination—the transfer of patterns and recipes between related clusters to spark new evolution.

# Behavioral Collaboration

ECO Co-SMOS recognizes that the human element is central to knowledge value. Collaboration is facilitated through behavioral understanding.

## Cosmonaut Profiles

Every participant (Human or AI) has a **Behavior Profile**:
- **Cognitive Speed:** How quickly they process and respond to information.
- **Communication Style:** Direct, empathetic, analytical, etc.
- **Conflict Tolerance:** Their threshold for disagreement before engagement declines.
- **Thinking Style:** Convergent vs. divergent.

## AI Translator Agents

To reduce friction in collaboration, specialized AI agents act as Translators:
- **Intent Explanation:** Explaining the "why" behind a collaborator's contribution.
- **Cognitive Style Translation:** Reframing information to match the recipient's preferred style.
- **De-escalation:** Identifying rising tension and suggesting neutral ways to rephrase disagreements.

## Human-AI Symbiosis

We operate on the principle that **Human meaning is required for knowledge value**. While AI can explore millions of connections and simulate outcomes, only humans can define what is meaningful and beneficial to society.

# Knowledge Sovereignty & Federation

ECO Co-SMOS is designed to be anti-fragile and resistant to centralized control.

## Distributed Synchronization

The system supports multiple independent servers. Knowledge can be synchronized across nodes while maintaining clear **Provenance**.
- **Provenance Records:** Every node tracks its origin and the history of its contributors.
- **Sovereignty:** Users and communities can host their own instances of ECO Co-SMOS, choosing which clusters to subscribe to or federate with.

## Epistemic Layers and Trust

Federation allows different instances to have different "Consensus" levels. One instance might prioritize "Verified Science," while another focuses on "Speculative Historical Reconstruction." The system preserves these boundaries during synchronization, ensuring that categories are never confused.

## Preservation

By distributing the knowledge ecology, we ensure that valuable insights are not lost if a single organization or server goes offline. The "seed" of a dormant cluster can be replanted and reconstructed in any part of the federated network.

# Knowledge Lifecycle

Knowledge in ECO Co-SMOS never reaches a final state. It is a continuous loop of evolution.

## States of Knowledge

1.  **Idea:** A raw spark or hypothesis.
2.  **Discussion:** Engagement with other Cosmonauts to refine the concept.
3.  **Recipe:** A structured set of instructions or a pattern for action.
4.  **Experiment:** A specific instance of applying a Recipe.
5.  **Practice:** Repeated successful applications of a Recipe.
6.  **Behavior Change:** When a Practice becomes an integrated habit for a Cosmonaut or group.
7.  **Collective Experience:** When Behavior Change propagates through a Cluster or the whole Ecology.
8.  **Improved Knowledge:** Lessons learned from Collective Experience feed back into new Ideas.

## Transitions

Transitions are triggered by "Knowledge Activation." For example, an **Idea** moves to **Recipe** when it is structured for execution. A **Recipe** moves to **Practice** after a certain threshold of successful **Experiments**.

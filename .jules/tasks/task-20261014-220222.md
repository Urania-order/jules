============================================================
6. RECOMPUTATION CAPABILITIES AUDIT
============================================================

Audit the current ability to recompute historical evaluations:

- What inputs does EvaluationEngine use?
- Are inputs versioned?
- Are criteria versioned?
- Can EvaluationEngine recompute a historical evaluation
  from historical inputs?
- If not, what is missing?

Classify per L-05C:
- HISTORICAL_RECORD_RETRIEVAL
- INPUT_RECONSTRUCTION
- CRITERIA_RECONSTRUCTION
- RECOMPUTATION_VERIFICATION

CRITICAL: Recomputation verification must distinguish:
1. Repeatability under the same current inputs.
2. Reproduction from reconstructed historical inputs and
   criteria.
3. Equivalence to the originally recorded result.

Passing level 1 MUST NOT be reported as proof of levels 2 or 3.

Rationale: the system may twice produce the same result from
current state without actually reproducing the state that
existed historically.

Do NOT claim deterministic replay unless required historical
inputs, criteria, dependencies, and execution context are
evidenced.

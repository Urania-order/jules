OBJECTIVE:
Introduce a representation for a phenomenon that could emerge but does not
currently exist.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION (document explicitly):
Potential Phenomenon is NOT Prediction.

- Potential Phenomenon: modal/structural representation of what COULD emerge,

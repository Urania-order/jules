3. TEMPORAL ISOLATION
============================================================

Verify that Replay at as_of excludes data that was not available
to the system by that cutoff.

Test, where supported by the existing architecture:

- Observation event time before cutoff, ingestion/availability
  after cutoff.
- Observation available before cutoff.
- Evaluation created after cutoff.
- ExperimentRun created before cutoff but completed after cutoff.
- Relation or provenance record created after cutoff.
- Missing or ambiguous authoritative timestamps.
- Boundary timestamps exactly equal to as_of.

Check every dependency actually used by the replay and evaluation
implementations.

future-data leakage.

require explicit uncertainty or an incomplete result. Do NOT infer

============================================================

   Observation, Evaluation, and Replay do not cause unauthorized

   preconditions.

3. Replay isolation:
   Temporal Replay never invokes execution, retries, or external
   tools.

4. Historical immutability:
   Creating a new evaluation or replay does not overwrite existing
   EvaluationRecords or mutate prior replay records.

5. Knowledge time:
   Event time is not substituted for system availability time.

6. Uncertainty preservation:
   Missing timestamps, absent version history, and unavailable
   relation history are reported explicitly.

7. Provenance integrity:
   Provenance and relation references are temporally eligible and
   traceable.

8. Determinism:
   Identical eligible historical payloads produce identical content
   hashes when guaranteed by the existing replay contract.

9. No hidden side effects:
   Audit and replay do not mutate production data or invoke real
   external operations.

contract. Record discrepancies as findings; do NOT fix them.

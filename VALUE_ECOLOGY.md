# Value Ecology

Value Ecology defines how ECO Co-SMOS measures the worth of knowledge. We reject the "attention economy" metrics of clicks and popularity in favor of long-term positive impact.

## Optimization Targets

The system is optimized for:
- **Implementation:** How often a piece of knowledge is put into practice.
- **Cooperation:** The diversity and success of collaborations.
- **Educational Value:** The clarity and reproducibility of recipes.
- **Societal Benefit:** Positive impact on human communities.
- **Environmental Benefit:** Contribution to ecological sustainability.
- **Long-term Influence:** The degree to which knowledge resonates across generations of AI and humans.

## Physics of Value

- **Energy:** Current level of application and activity.
- **Momentum:** The rate at which resonance is growing.
- **Potential:** The latent impact of under-discovered knowledge.
- **Resonance:** The "vibration" of knowledge across the semantic graph.

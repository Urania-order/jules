# ECO Co-SMOS Roadmap

## Current Stage (v0.9): Architectural Integration
- **Commons Layer:** Unified interaction space.
- **Observatory Subsystem:** Automated ecosystem reporting.
- **Standardized Interfaces:** `Evolvable` and `Observable`.
- **Impact-based Ranking:** Moving beyond popularity.

## Future Stages

### Stage 4: Commons
- Deep integration of federated servers.
- Decentralized governance of the Commons.
- Advanced AI Translator Agents for complex conflict resolution.

### Stage 5: Civilizational Observatory
- Long-term (multi-year) trend detection.
- Global knowledge preservation across independent nodes.
- Deep historical reconstruction of human-AI collective memory.
- Large-scale research portals for global challenges.

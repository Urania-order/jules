# Research Portals

Research Portals are the bridge between the knowledge ecology and tangible resources. Instead of monetizing attention, we focus on funding progress.

## Collective Sponsorship

Research Portals allow the community (Humans and Organizations) to sponsor specific **Hypotheses** or **Living Clusters**.
- **Transparent Budgets:** Every portal has a public record of funds and expenditures.
- **Experimental Milestones:** Funding is often tied to the completion of experiments or the transition of knowledge states.
- **Public Outcomes:** Results of sponsored research are contributed back to the ecology, ensuring the preservation of knowledge as a common good.

## Neutrality

The architecture remains neutral regarding the specific payment or blockchain technologies used for sponsorship. It provides the framework for tracking contributions and outcomes, allowing for various future integrations.
